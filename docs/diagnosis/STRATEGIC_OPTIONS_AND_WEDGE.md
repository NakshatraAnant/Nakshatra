## The wedge
**[Hypothesis]**
- **Line:** "See how you two fit — calculated, explained, and only with both of you."
- **Who:** Indian and diaspora 18–30-year-olds who use astrology playfully, often about relationships.
- **Entry:** a free Cosmic ID with no account. **Core:** a consented two-person preview. **Revenue:** a full pair report and a gift report, paid once.

## Why Nakshatra can win this specific gap
1. **[Observed]** `bonds.ts` already has consent scopes, revocation and public share links. Competitors such as AstroSage matching do not lead with consent [Inference].
2. **[Observed]** The engine computes both Vedic (Guna Milan, Manglik with exceptions) and Western systems. Few self-serve products show both side by side [Inference].
3. **[Observed]** The "calculated, not generated" no-AI stance is real and documented. That is credible against AI astrology, *if* the hand-written content is good.

## Why it could fail
- **[Inference]** It is easy to copy. AstroSage or Co-Star could ship a consented pair report within a quarter.
- **[Hypothesis]** Willingness to pay for compatibility may be close to zero when free Guna Milan exists.
- **[Inference]** Content quality is a writing problem, not an engineering problem. The team has not yet shown it can write at this level.
- **[Inference]** The name is generic and contested.
