# Monetization playbook

_2026-10-25. All 22 models are scored in `MONETIZATION_SCORECARD.csv`. Each is rated 1–5 on eight criteria, for a maximum of 40._

## Principles (non-negotiable)
1. **Never sell or share identifiable birth data.** Not even "anonymised" data sold for marketing. Birth date, time and place can re-identify a person [Inference].
2. **No fear-based upsells.** No "your Manglik dosha requires a remedy". No "Sade Sati warning, buy protection".
3. **No dark patterns.** Prices are shown up front. There are no auto-renewing trials in the MVP. Cancellation is one click and refunds are simple. This is in line with the CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines, 2023 [External].
4. **No fake scarcity, countdowns or false social proof.** Nakshatra has no testimonials, so it shows none.
5. **Pay once first.** Subscriptions only after retention is proven.

## Five monetization experiments
| ID | Experiment | Design | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Pair report price test | Randomised ₹149 / ₹199 / ₹299 shown to preview completers. Real report, real charge | ≥1% at any price; pick the price with the best revenue per preview |
| M2 | Gift option | Add "Gift this to a pair" after purchase and on the landing page | ≥15% of paid reports are gifts |
| M3 | Family explainer | A separate landing page plus 10 J2 interviewees given a real report. Ask for payment at the end; they may decline | ≥5 of 10 pay, or ≥2% conversion online |
| M4 | Print pilot | Manual fulfilment through one print-on-demand partner for the first 20 orders, stated honestly as "made to order, ships in X days" | ≥2% of report buyers add a print |
| M5 | Creator codes | 10 micro-creators, unique codes, 15% share | ≥1 creator drives ≥50 completers and ≥5 sales |

Every experiment delivers a real product at the price charged. None uses fake doors.

## Models explicitly rejected
| Model | Reason |
|---|---|
| Expert marketplace commission | Subsidy war, trust and regulatory risk |
| Gemstones and remedies | Remedy-claim risk, possibly under the Drugs and Magic Remedies Act (needs counsel) [Inference] |
| Advertising | Low revenue at small scale; erodes trust |
| Dating and matchmaking premium | Incumbents already own this, and it carries a heavy safety burden |
| Data sales | Prohibited |

## When subscriptions come back
Nakshatra+ (₹199/month, ₹1,999/year, as seeded) returns only when all three hold:
1. At least 20% of paying users return unprompted within 30 days.
2. At least 90 days of distinct content exists.
3. Cancellation and refund flows have been reviewed against the CCPA guidelines.
