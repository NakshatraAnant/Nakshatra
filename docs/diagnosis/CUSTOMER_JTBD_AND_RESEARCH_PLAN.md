## Interview plan (15–25 interviews, days 2–14)
- **Recruit:**
  - 10 for J1/J3: aged 18–30, have used any astrology app or reel in the last 3 months, and are in or were recently in a relationship or situationship.
  - 5–10 for J2: had a kundli discussion about a real match in the last 2 years.
  - 3–5 non-believers who use astrology "for fun", as a control.
- **Channels:**
  - personal network (second-degree, not friends)
  - college societies
  - two Reddit/Discord communities (by asking moderators first)
  - diaspora groups
  - no paid panels before interview 10
- **Consent:** verbal and recorded consent, notes stored without names, and no birth data collected during interviews.

## Script (problem interview, 30 minutes, no product shown)
1. "Tell me about the last time astrology came up in a relationship, a crush, a partner or a family match." Ask for the specific story.
2. "What did you do? Which app, person or site? What happened next?"
3. "What was annoying, embarrassing or missing?"
4. "Have you ever paid for anything astrology-related? What, how much, and why that one?"
5. "Have you ever sent something astrology-related to someone? What was it, and what did they say?"
6. (J2) "When the Guna number came up, who explained it? Did it change any decision?"
7. "If you could check compatibility only when the other person agreed, would that matter to you?"
8. Closing: "Who else should I talk to?"

**Rules:**
- No pitching.
- Don't mention prices before the person does.
- Record exact quotes.
- Write each interview up within 24 hours, with each finding labelled as a quote or as an interpretation.

## Synthesis criteria (feeds Gate 1)
- J1 or J2 is named as a real, recent behaviour by at least 8 of 15 people.
- At least 5 people describe paying, or say unprompted that they would pay, for something comparable.
- At least 50% care about consent and "calculated, explained".
- If J2 is stronger than J1, switch the wedge variant (see `STRATEGIC_OPTIONS_AND_WEDGE.md`).

## Content blind test (E02)
Show 20 people two unlabelled texts for their own placement: the current rule text and the rewritten text. Ask each person:
- which feels more like them
- which they would screenshot
- whether either feels fake

The pass mark: the rewrite wins with at least 70% of people.
