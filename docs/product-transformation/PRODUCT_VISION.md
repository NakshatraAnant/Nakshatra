# Product vision

## Central promise
**Nakshatra turns your birth moment into an identity you can understand, keep and share — and into honest, consented ways to understand the people in your life.**

## Thesis
- **[Inference]** People come to astrology for self-recognition and for relationships. Indian demand concentrates on relationships and marriage (`docs/diagnosis`).
- **What Nakshatra can own** is the combination of three things:
  1. Calculated, explained identity in both Vedic and Western systems. Calculated, not generated.
  2. A recognisable artefact: an archetype plus a sigil drawn from the chart itself.
  3. Relationship features that run only with both people's consent.

## Audiences, in order
1. **Indian and diaspora 18–32-year-olds** who use astrology for reflection and conversation, often about relationships. This is the primary audience, and the only one with dedicated surfaces today.
2. **People facing a family kundli conversation** who want the Guna score explained, not delivered as a verdict. Secondary; needs its own validation.
3. **Enthusiasts** who want the full chart, dashas and Panchang. Already served by the Chart and Calendar pages.

## Architecture of the universe
| Layer | Role | Today |
|---|---|---|
| A. Identity foundation | Birth profile, both charts, methodology, privacy | Strong (existing) |
| B. Cosmic identity | Archetype, sigil, three Vedic layers, share cards | **Built this phase** |
| C. Daily experience | A grounded reason to return | Exists; content too thin to promote |
| D. Relationships | Consented Bonds, Guna Milan explained, dimensions | Exists; invitee still needs an account |
| E. Collections | Saved insights, journal, timeline, reports | Exists |
| F. Community | Feed, groups | Built, but held in Labs until there is enough network density |
| G. Learning and utilities | Lessons, Panchang, festivals | Exists |
| H. Services and commerce | Reports, experts, shop, weddings | Reports kept and labelled honestly; the others are in Labs |

## Rules every feature must pass
A feature must name:
1. its outcome
2. why Nakshatra (calculated, consented, culturally fluent)
3. how it connects to identity or Bonds
4. how it deepens the relationship with the user
5. how it can earn money without dark patterns

Anything that cannot do this stays in Labs or is deferred.

## What Nakshatra is not
- Not a horoscope feed.
- Not a fortune-telling or remedy shop.
- Not an AI oracle.
- Not a dating app that happens to show signs.
