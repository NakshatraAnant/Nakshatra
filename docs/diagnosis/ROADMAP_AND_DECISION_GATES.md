# Roadmap and decision gates

_2026-10-25. Every step is reversible. Modules are hidden with flags or taken out of the navigation, never deleted. No database reset. No architecture change._

## Days 1–2: decide and freeze
- The owner accepts or rejects the wedge, recorded in `docs/DECISIONS_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md`.
- Feature-flag non-wedge modules out of the navigation for new users. Small and reversible; do it only after the decision.
- Start the trademark clearance search.
- Book interviews (`CUSTOMER_JTBD_AND_RESEARCH_PLAN.md`).

## Days 3–30: learn
**Work:**
- 15–25 problem interviews.
- Content sprint: distinct Vedic and Western texts for the Sun/Rashi, Moon/Nakshatra and Rising/Lagna layers, a Hinglish version, and the pair-report outline.
- Run E02 (content blind test).
- Build the no-signup Cosmic ID and a landing page with a labelled sample.
- Funnel view in admin.
- Razorpay merchant account (owner KYC).

**Gate 1 (day 30). All three must hold:**
- J1 or J2 confirmed by at least 8 of 15 interviewees.
- At least 5 people describe paying for, or unprompted say they would pay for, something comparable.
- The rewritten content wins the blind test with at least 70% of readers.

**If it fails:** re-interview with the J2 segment; if that also fails, apply the stop criteria.

## Days 31–60: first money
**Work:**
- Consented Bond preview, with no account needed for the invitee.
- Paid pair report and the ₹149/199/299 price test (M1).
- Gift option (M2).
- Seed 10 creators (M5).

**Gate 2 (day 60). Both must hold:**
- At least 300 preview completers.
- At least 1% paid, or at least 0.5% and rising after one content iteration.
- Refunds at or below 3%.

**If it fails:** one more content iteration; if it still fails, pivot to D2 (family explainer) or stop.

## Days 61–90: loop
**Work:**
- Share-card iteration.
- Family explainer test (M3).
- Print pilot (M4).
- Event-based return notifications (opt-in).

**Gate 3 (day 90). At least one must hold:**
- k ≥ 0.3.
- At least 15% of paid reports are gifts.
- Revenue covers content cost.

**If it passes:** invest in content depth; consider an annual pair report.

**If it fails:** apply the stop/pivot criteria in §23 of the main report.

## Never unfreeze a module because the code exists
Each hidden module needs its own evidence before it returns:

| Module | Condition for return |
|---|---|
| Daily | At least 20% of payers return unprompted within 30 days, and at least 90 days of distinct content exist |
| Experts | Repeated user demand for human help, and a supply partner |
| Dating, matchmaking | A distribution partner, plus a safety and moderation budget |
| Weddings | A separate business case |
