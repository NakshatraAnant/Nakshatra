**[Inference]** Indian astrology revenue is dominated by **live human consultation**, paid per minute and acquired with first-session subsidies. The self-serve calculation layer is given away free (AstroSage). Nakshatra has no supply-side advantage in consultations, so it should not compete there.

## Consumer attitudes
- **Pew, US, 21 May 2025:** 28% of adults consult astrology, tarot or fortune tellers at least yearly. 27% believe astrology. 43% of women aged 18–49 believe. Most say they use it for fun.
- **India:** I found no nationally representative survey of astrology attitudes, and no credible YouGov India study **[Unknown]**.

## Competitor positioning map
**[Inference]**
| | Self-serve | Human-led |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | Co-Star, CHANI, AstroSage free tools | Astrotalk, InstaAstro |
| **Relationship** | The Pattern (Bonds), Co-Star friends, AstroSage matching (free, utilitarian), Tinder (inside dating). **Gap: consented, explainable, beautiful, India-priced** | Family astrologer; marketplace consultations on "marriage" |

That gap is narrow and copyable. Nakshatra can defend it only with content quality, trust, and the share/pair loop.
