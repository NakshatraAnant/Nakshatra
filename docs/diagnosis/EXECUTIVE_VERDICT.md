# Executive verdict

_2026-10-25. Main report: `NAKSHATRA_BRUTAL_PRODUCT_DIAGNOSIS.md`. Evidence labels are defined there._

## Verdict
**Continue, narrowed hard, behind a 90-day validation gate.** Nakshatra is an engineering success and a business non-event:
- **[Verified]** It works: 343 routes, 129 tables; typecheck, lint and 118/118 tests pass, re-run today.
- **[Verified]** It has no users: 10 seed or test accounts, ₹0 revenue, no interviews.
- **[Founder]/[Inference]** The prior strategy report warned against building the full platform, and it was built anyway.

## Five findings
1. **[Verified]** Content, not code, is the bottleneck:
   - about 6,545 unique words of interpretation
   - Western and Vedic layers share body text
   - 0 Hindi bodies
   - daily pools repeat within about two weeks
2. **[Observed]** The paid ₹299 Vedic report mostly repackages interpretations the user already saw free.
3. **[Observed]** Time to first value is about five steps: signup, intro, birth form and interests. The landing page shows no sample output. _(Corrected: email verification does not block the first result.)_
4. **[Observed]** The fast path to compatibility, a private Bond, uses another person's birth data on the user's own attestation that they have permission. The verified-consent path forces that person to sign up. _(Corrected: an attestation checkbox already exists in the form and the API.)_
5. **[External]** Astrotalk and InstaAstro own consultations. AstroSage gives away calculation for free. Tinder ships astrology in dating. Co-Star now has Midjourney money. Most of what was built competes with funded incumbents.

## Strongest opportunity and wedge
**[Hypothesis]** A consented, explainable two-person compatibility report, Vedic and Western, priced for India, entered through a free no-signup Cosmic ID. It is paid once, and it can be gifted.

## Three surviving features
1. Instant Cosmic ID, no account needed
2. Consented Bond preview, where the invitee needs no account
3. Paid full pair report, with gifting

## Hide (do not delete)
Daily briefing, XP, social, messaging, dating, matchmaking, weddings, experts, courses, physical shop, subscription, and private third-party Bonds.
